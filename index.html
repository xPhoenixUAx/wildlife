Useful answers

Questions before the next step

Clear expectations help homeowners have a more useful first conversation with an independent provider.

What does Wildlife Match do?
Wildlife Match organizes your request and may introduce it to independent providers serving your area.
Does Wildlife Match perform inspections or removal?
No. Wildlife Match is a referral platform, not a contractor. Providers independently assess the situation and perform any agreed work.
Is a provider response guaranteed?
No. Responses depend on your location, provider availability and the details of the request.
How much will the work cost?
Independent providers set their own pricing after discussing or assessing the scope. Ask for a written estimate before work begins.
What details should I share?
Describe where and when you notice activity, visible signs and any photos taken from a safe position. Do not enter unsafe areas or approach wildlife.
What should I verify before hiring a provider?
Ask about licensing, insurance, methods, species-specific rules, the written scope, follow-up and warranties.
