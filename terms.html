Your provider-verification responsibilities

Before hiring or allowing work to begin, you are solely responsible for evaluating the provider. Appropriate verification may include:

  • Confirming the legal business name and current contact information.
  • Checking licenses, registrations, insurance, bonding, permits and certifications required for the location and scope.

  • Requesting a written inspection record, scope, exclusions, price, schedule and warranty terms.
