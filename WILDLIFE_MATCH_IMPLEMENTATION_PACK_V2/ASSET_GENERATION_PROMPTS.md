# ASSET GENERATION PROMPTS

Use these prompts when image generation is available. Generate each as a separate asset without text, logos or watermark. Keep subjects realistic and anatomically correct.

## Shared suffix

`Natural documentary/editorial photography, bright warm daylight, realistic North American residential property, restrained premium color grade, friendly and reassuring, no people unless requested, no text, no logo, no watermark, no trapped or injured animal, no horror, no glowing eyes.`

## Homepage hero

- Garden: `A healthy gray squirrel calmly standing in a lush landscaped residential garden, three-quarter view, house edge softly visible, usable wide crop.`
- Wall: `Close architectural crop of warm light-colored residential siding, window edge and flowering shrubs, midday sunlight, vertical framing.`
- Roof: `Small songbird resting on a clean asphalt-shingle roof edge against blue sky and leafy trees, wide horizontal framing.`
- Interior: `Warm sunlit contemporary living room with ivory sofa, terracotta cushion, wood table and plants, no animal, vertical-to-square framing.`

## Services

- Wildlife removal: `Healthy raccoon outdoors near flowering shrubs beside a home, calm alert posture, landscape crop.`
- Attic: `Bright clean timber attic while a properly protected worker carefully removes or vacuums loose material, face not identifiable, wide crop.`
- Sealing: `Clean residential soffit and vent detail after professional-grade exclusion/sealing, no visible branding, long horizontal crop.`

## Process and clues

- Tracks: `Subtle raccoon tracks in soft soil along a residential foundation, plants framing the scene, tall crop.`
- Inspection: `Worker in neutral protective clothing safely inspecting a roof edge from an exterior ladder in daylight, face not identifiable.`
- Vent clue: `Slightly displaced exterior soffit vent, clear but not catastrophic damage.`
- Nesting clue: `Small quantity of nesting material collected near an exterior architectural edge, daylight, clean contextual photo.`

## Request preparation and about

- Table: `Top-down modern documentation scene with smartphone showing a roof photo, flashlight, work gloves and simple notes on warm ivory surface; no readable personal data.`
- Safe photo: `Homeowner photographing a roofline from ground level, back to camera, safe distance, bright garden.`
- Conversation: `Homeowner and neutral home-service provider discussing an exterior area beside a house, backs or profiles, friendly daylight, no branded uniforms.`

## Service pages

Generate four separate images per service. Never reuse the homepage hero. Match the reference crop roles: tall subject, wide exterior context, narrow material detail and one supporting section photograph.
