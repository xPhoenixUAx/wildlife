# CONTENT AND PAGE MAP

## Product language

Wildlife Match organizes homeowner requests and may introduce them to independent providers. It is not a contractor and does not perform, price, supervise, inspect or guarantee work.

Use: `may introduce`, `independent providers`, `provider response and availability vary`, `ask providers`, `verify`.

Never use: `our technicians`, `our wildlife experts`, `we remove`, `we inspect`, `we seal`, `trusted providers`, `guaranteed match`, `free estimate`, `same-day service`.

## Service page 1 — `wildlife-removal.html`

H1: **Wildlife Removal**

Five required sections:

1. Interlocked hero from reference 09.
2. **Situations providers may assess**: wildlife seen entering an opening; recurring attic/ceiling sounds; nesting near vents or roof edges; droppings or odor; animal activity near crawlspace or foundation.
3. **What an inspection may cover**: entry/access areas; species and seasonal considerations; interior signs; exclusion opportunities; cleanup/repair needs.
4. **Questions to ask before work begins**: method, legal/species rules, young/nesting animals, follow-up visits, written scope, warranty.
5. Shared request form with service preselected.

## Service page 2 — `attic-cleanup-restoration.html`

H1: **Attic Cleanup & Restoration**

Five required sections:

1. Unique interlocked hero using clean attic and material photography.
2. **When cleanup may be discussed**: nesting material; droppings; odor; disturbed insulation; staining/moisture; debris after removal.
3. **What the scope may include**: assessment, material removal, sanitation approach, insulation discussion, ventilation/moisture, access limitations.
4. **Questions to ask**: containment, PPE, disposal, cleaning products, insulation measurement, photos/documentation, warranty.
5. Shared request form with service preselected.

Do not promise sterilization, disease elimination or restored R-value without provider assessment.

## Service page 3 — `entry-point-sealing-prevention.html`

H1: **Entry Point Sealing & Prevention**

Five required sections:

1. Unique interlocked hero using soffit, roof-edge and vent photography.
2. **Common exterior areas**: soffits/fascia; roof vents; chimney intersections; utility penetrations; foundation vents; gaps at additions.
3. **Planning the exclusion work**: identify species; confirm animals are out; choose compatible materials; preserve ventilation/drainage; schedule follow-up.
4. **Questions to ask**: material, one-way doors, seasonal timing, repair responsibility, photographs, warranty, follow-up.
5. Shared request form with service preselected.
