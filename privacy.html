How information moves through the Wildlife Match introduction process.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the Wildlife Match website collects, why it is used, when it may be shared and the choices available to visitors.

Last updated August 29, 2026

Scope and operator

This Privacy Policy applies to this Wildlife Match website and the request-introduction functions provided through it. The website is operated by Site Operator, referred to in this policy as “Wildlife Match,” “we,” “us” or “our.”

Wildlife Match is an independent referral platform. It does not perform wildlife removal, attic restoration, construction, inspection or exclusion work. Information may be used to organize a request and, where the user has agreed, introduce that request to independent local providers.

Information we collect

Information you provide

The request form may collect:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your ZIP or postal code and selected service category.
  • Your written description of the issue, timing, location and observations.
  • Photographs or other files you choose to upload, including information visible in those files and any metadata they retain.
  • Your consent selection and records associated with submitting the request.

The current form does not request a telephone number, payment-card number, government identifier or account password. Do not place sensitive personal, financial or medical information in the free-text field or photographs.

Information collected automatically

When a browser communicates with the site, hosting and security systems may receive technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, requested page, referring page, date and time, server response, session identifier and security or rate-limiting events. The current site also stores a cookie-choice value in browser local storage.

Sources of information

We receive information directly from users who complete the request form or contact us. Technical information is produced by the user’s browser, the web server and infrastructure used to deliver and protect the site. If another person submits information for a household or property, that person is responsible for having authority to do so.

Independent providers may later tell us whether they accepted an introduction or provide limited operational feedback about request status. Their own collection of information during calls, inspections, estimates or service work is governed by their separate practices.

How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • Receive, organize, review and respond to a service request.
  • Identify independent providers that may serve the area or requested category.
  • Share request details with potential providers when the user has consented.
  • Communicate administrative information about a request or this website.
  • Operate, maintain, debug and improve site functionality.
  • Protect users, providers and the site from fraud, abuse, malicious files and security threats.
  • Maintain appropriate business, consent, security and dispute records.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

Submitting a request does not guarantee an introduction, response, estimate, appointment or completed service.

When information is shared

Independent providers

When a user agrees to provider sharing, relevant request details and attachments may be provided to one or more independent businesses so they can decide whether to respond. A provider may contact the user using the submitted email address. Providers are not employees or agents of Wildlife Match and may have their own privacy notices.

Operational vendors

Information may be processed by vendors that support hosting, email delivery, file storage, security, debugging or professional services. These vendors should receive only the information reasonably needed for their role and be subject to appropriate contractual or professional obligations.

Legal and organizational disclosures

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process or a valid governmental request; protect rights, safety or property; investigate abuse; establish or defend legal claims; or support a merger, financing, acquisition, sale or reorganization involving the website operator.

The site does not load optional analytics or advertising scripts. If targeted advertising, analytics, sale or other materially different sharing is introduced, this policy and the consent controls will be updated before those technologies are used.

Retention and deletion

Information should be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. Relevant factors may include the time needed to handle a request, maintain consent and referral records, prevent repeated abuse, resolve disputes, meet accounting or legal obligations and preserve evidence of site operation.

Different records may have different retention periods. When information is no longer needed, it should be deleted, anonymized or securely disposed of where reasonably practicable. Backup copies may persist for a limited period until overwritten under ordinary backup schedules.

Security

We use administrative, technical and organizational measures intended to protect information appropriate to its nature and the risks involved. Measures may include access restrictions, transport encryption, session protections, request validation, file controls, logging and vendor review.

No internet transmission, email, storage platform or security measure can guarantee absolute security. Users should avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive information and should contact us if they believe information submitted through the site has been misused.

Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live and subject to applicable exceptions, you may be able to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information; receive a portable copy; object to or restrict certain processing; withdraw consent for future sharing; or appeal a decision concerning a request.

To protect users, we may need to verify the requester’s identity and authority. We may retain limited records where necessary for security, legal compliance or to document that a request was completed. An authorized agent may submit a request where applicable, subject to verification.

Browser storage choices

You may accept or reject optional storage through the cookie banner and may clear stored choices in browser settings. The current site does not load optional analytics or advertising scripts. More detail appears in the Cookie Policy.

Children’s privacy

The site is intended for adults seeking information about property-related services. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes a child submitted information, they may contact us to request review and deletion.

Independent providers and third-party sites

Once an independent provider receives request information or collects additional information directly, that provider determines its own practices for calls, estimates, inspections, contracts and service records. Wildlife Match does not control those practices.

The website may link to third-party sites. Their privacy practices, content and security are governed by their own terms and notices. Users should review a provider’s privacy practices before providing additional sensitive information.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when site features, information practices, providers, laws or operational needs change. The revised version will be posted with a new “Last updated” date. Where required, a more prominent notice or renewed consent will be provided before a material change takes effect.
